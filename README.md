## Research Focus

My work is centered on the following questions:

- How do biological foundation models organize sequence-derived information in latent space?

- Can stochastic and geometric models help interpret local movement, uncertainty, and drift in biological representation spaces?

## Selected Research Projects

### Latent Geometry Audit of AntigenLM Influenza Representations

Repository: [`latent_geometry`](https://github.com/cmorregof/latent_geometry)

A geometric audit of AntigenLM latent representations for Influenza A HA/NA sequences. The project studies whether learned embeddings preserve molecular similarity, temporal coherence, intrinsic low-dimensional structure, and evolutionary-taxonomic neighborhood enrichment.

The repository includes analysis code, aggregate outputs, robustness checks, random embedding baselines, clade-label enrichment analyses, figures, and a manuscript package.

**Keywords:** AntigenLM, Influenza A, HA/NA, latent geometry, representation learning, biological foundation models, AI for Biology.

---

### FluGenome3D

Repository: [`FluGenome3D`](https://github.com/cmorregof/FluGenome3D)

A deployable research interface for derived Influenza A HA/NA artifacts. FluGenome3D connects sequence-context metrics, deterministic tokenization, AntigenLM latent geometry, public structure-alignment QC, and safe aggregate visualization.

The project is designed around a governance-first data layer: restricted raw biological records stay local, while deployable views use derived, aggregate, and hash-based artifacts.

**Keywords:** research systems, scientific visualization, Influenza A, AntigenLM, latent atlas, structure QC, safe data interfaces.

---

### AntigenLM Thesis / AntigenSDE

Repository: [`antigenlm-thesis`](https://github.com/cmorregof/antigenlm-thesis)

Master’s thesis work around AntigenLM reimplementation, Influenza HA/NA representation analysis, and stochastic latent-dynamics extensions.

The broader AntigenSDE direction explores stochastic modeling over biological sequence-model embeddings, with the goal of studying local movement, uncertainty, and drift in latent representation spaces.

**Status:** active research thesis repository.

**Keywords:** AntigenLM, stochastic differential equations, latent dynamics, representation analysis, Influenza, scientific ML.

---

### HantaVec

Repository: [`hanta_vec`](https://github.com/cmorregof/hanta_vec)

A latent-space audit of pre-trained ESM-2 embeddings for Orthohantavirus glycoproteins. The project evaluates whether protein language model embeddings preserve species-level structure despite compressing pairwise sequence similarities.

The pipeline includes NCBI data curation, ESM-2 embedding extraction, PCA/UMAP visualization, sequence-identity baselines, conservation analysis, caching, and reproducibility manifests.

**Keywords:** ESM-2, Orthohantavirus, protein language models, species clustering, embedding audit, computational biology.
