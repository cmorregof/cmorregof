I study the afterimage of biological foundation models.

A viral sequence enters as text.  
A model compresses it into coordinates.  
Somewhere in that compression, molecular similarity, evolutionary memory, temporal drift, and taxonomic structure either survive or disappear.

My work is about auditing that space.

Not only training models.  
Not only plotting embeddings.  
But asking what kind of biological geometry remains when life is translated into latent coordinates.

## Research Objects

### `latent_geometry`

**Geometric audit of AntigenLM representations for Influenza A HA/NA.**

This project studies whether AntigenLM latent spaces preserve biologically meaningful structure across molecular similarity, temporal organization, subtype behavior, intrinsic dimension, and evolutionary-taxonomic neighborhoods.

It treats the embedding space as an object of scientific inspection.

<p>
  <a href="https://github.com/cmorregof/latent_geometry"><strong>Repository</strong></a>
</p>

---

### `AntigenSDE`

**Stochastic modeling of antigenic drift over learned biological representations.**

AntigenSDE explores continuous-time latent dynamics for Influenza A evolution, combining biological sequence embeddings with stochastic differential equations, competitive fitness landscapes, Monte Carlo simulation, and uncertainty-aware trajectories.

The goal is not to decorate embeddings with dynamics, but to ask whether latent biological spaces can sustain a useful mathematical model of motion.

---

### `FluGenome3D`

**A visual research atlas for derived Influenza A artifacts.**

FluGenome3D is a deployable interface for inspecting sequence-context metrics, latent geometry, structural QC signals, and derived biological artifacts without exposing restricted raw data.

It sits between research notebook, atlas, and instrument panel.

<p>
  <a href="https://github.com/cmorregof/FluGenome3D"><strong>Repository</strong></a>
</p>

---

### `hanta_vec`

**Latent-space audit of Orthohantavirus glycoprotein embeddings.**

A compact audit of ESM-2 representations for viral glycoproteins, studying species-level structure, sequence-identity baselines, dimensional compression, and conservation patterns.

A second signal that the research program generalizes beyond a single virus or model family.

<p>
  <a href="https://github.com/cmorregof/hanta_vec"><strong>Repository</strong></a>
</p>
