## `about.me`

I am a mathematician and computer scientist working at the intersection of **mathematical modeling**, **machine learning**, and **scientific computing**.

My current work explores how modern AI systems encode structure: from **latent spaces in biological sequence models** to **agentic workflows for theorem proving and formal reasoning**. I care about models that are not only powerful, but also interpretable, auditable, and mathematically grounded.

> I like systems where geometry, probability, and computation quietly conspire to explain something real.

---

## `current.focus`

* 🧬 **Latent geometry of biological embeddings**
  Studying molecular, temporal, and taxonomic structure in sequence-model representations.

* 🧠 **Scientific machine learning**
  Building computational pipelines for simulation, inference, and model validation.

* 🧩 **Agentic AI for mathematics**
  Exploring LLM-based agents for formal theorem proving, especially around Isabelle and combinatorics.

* 📐 **Stochastic modeling & applied mathematics**
  Working with diffusion processes, jump models, numerical methods, and probabilistic reasoning.
